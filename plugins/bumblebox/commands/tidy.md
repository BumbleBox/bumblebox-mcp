description: Tidy my inbox with BumbleBox — archive, file or trash what I approve
---

Use the BumbleBox tools to help me tidy my inbox.
List my recent inbox emails with `search_emails` and group them: to answer, to keep, to archive
or file (`list_folders` gives the folders), and noise for the Trash.
Show me the groups and act only on the ones I approve, with `archive_email`, `move_email` or
`trash_email`. Tell me which trash requests BumbleBox turned into proposals for me to confirm.
Everything can be undone with `restore_to_inbox`; never claim an email was deleted for good.
